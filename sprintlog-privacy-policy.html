SprintLog — Focus Sessions

The short version

SprintLog is a focus timer and productivity app. It is designed to let you manage focus sessions, tasks, session history, and statistics on your device. This policy describes the intended data practices and must match the app build actually distributed to users.

1. Information handled by SprintLog

Depending on how you use the app, SprintLog may handle information you enter or create, such as:

  • Tasks and task descriptions.
  • Focus-session durations and completion records.
  • Session history and productivity statistics.
  • App preferences and settings.

These categories describe the app's intended core features. The developer must verify the released code and included software components before relying on these statements.

2. How information is used

This information is used to provide focus timing, task management, session history, statistics, and app preferences. It should not be used for unrelated purposes unless the app's practices and this policy are updated accordingly.

3. Local storage, collection, and sharing

SprintLog is designed to work offline and without requiring an account. Its core information is intended to be stored locally on your device, rather than sent to a SprintLog-operated server.

The intended app design does not include advertising or third-party analytics. However, operating-system services, device backups, or included third-party libraries may behave separately. The developer must inspect the current build and dependencies to confirm whether any information is transmitted to third parties. This policy must be corrected if the implementation differs from these statements.

4. Retention and deletion

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling may remove local app data. Copies may remain in device backups or other services managed by your device or operating-system provider.

SprintLog does not describe a developer-operated account or server for deleting locally stored session records. Use available in-app controls and your device settings to manage local information.

5. Security

Keeping information on your device can reduce the need to transmit it to a remote service. Protect your device with a screen lock and current system updates. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and backup security depends on the services and settings you use.

6. Permissions

SprintLog should request only permissions needed for its actual features. Consult the app's current Android permission declarations and behavior for the definitive list. If the app adds permissions or services that access information, this policy should be updated to explain them.

7. Children's privacy and target audience

SprintLog is a general productivity app. Its declared target audience in Google Play should accurately reflect whom the app is designed for. If the app is directed to children or changes its audience, the app's data practices and this policy must be reviewed for applicable requirements.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when SprintLog's features, included components, or data practices change. The effective date will be revised when a new version is published.
